Community Giving

At Meridian Group, we partner with local charities to support the causes our people care about. Use the Donate button at the bottom right of this page to give to our featured campaign.

Our commitment

Each year, Meridian Group matches employee donations dollar for dollar and volunteers over 4,000 hours with community partners across Singapore. Our featured campaign changes quarterly and is selected by our people.

Donations are processed securely by Giving.sg, Singapore's national giving platform. Meridian Group does not collect or store any payment information.
